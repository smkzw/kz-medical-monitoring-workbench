User and product semantics:
1. A candidate is not approved by the AI. It is proposed by AI and becomes
   authoritative working content when the medical author selects it.
2. Selecting an AI candidate must atomically:
   - verify the candidate, source, target anchor, working-copy revision, and
     current StudyDefinition authority;
   - record `user_confirmed` with actor/time/source/candidate identity;
   - apply the selected text/table content to the working copy;
   - advance the working-copy revision;
   - create the audit event.
3. There is no intermediate
   `accepted_pending_medical_approval` user workflow and no separate
   “医学批准” click for the same candidate.
4. Selecting one candidate does not automatically claim the whole chapter is
   finished. The author can continue editing other content.
5. A chapter-level “确认本章定稿” action freezes the exact current working-copy
   revision as the export snapshot. It is version confirmation by the same
   author, not approval by another role.
6. Any later edit, StudyDefinition change, source change, quarantine, or
   blocking consistency finding invalidates only the current chapter-final
   status while preserving its historical snapshot and audit record.
7. Formal DOCX export requires every included chapter to have a current
   author-confirmed snapshot and all existing quality/source/consistency gates
   to pass. It does not require a separate Approval Center action.
8. Manual author edits remain normal working drafts until the author confirms
   the chapter version; they do not require per-paragraph approval.
9. Existing historical status values may be migrated or mapped for backward
   compatibility, but public API/UI must expose one unambiguous current
   semantics. Do not silently discard historical decisions.
